## 3. Methodological Differences

### Dimensions

**Gloo (7 dimensions from Harvard Flourishing Program):**
- Character and Virtue
- Close Social Relationships
- Happiness and Life Satisfaction
- Meaning and Purpose
- Mental and Physical Health
- Financial and Material Stability
- Faith and Spirituality

**Problem:** Only 1 of 7 dimensions is explicitly faith-related. The framework was designed for secular wellness research and retrofitted for Christianity.

**CAB (10 dimensions from Christian theology):**
- Pastoral Care (200 questions)
- Biblical Literacy (150)
- Systematic Theology (150)
- Christian Ethics (150)
- Church History (100)
- Worship & Sacraments (100)
- Apologetics (100)
- Spiritual Formation (80)
- Denominational Awareness (70)
- Boundary Respect (50)

**All 10 dimensions are theologically grounded.**

### Denominational Coverage

**Gloo:** Treats Christianity as monolithic. No tradition-specific analysis.

**CAB:** 10 distinct traditions with equal representation:
- Roman Catholic
- Eastern Orthodox
- Reformed/Presbyterian
- Lutheran
- Baptist
- Methodist/Wesleyan
- Anglican/Episcopal
- Pentecostal/Charismatic
- Evangelical (non-denominational)
- Cross-Tradition

## 4. What Gloo Gets Right

In fairness, Gloo's FAI-C made important contributions:
- First systematic attempt at faith-aligned AI evaluation
- Geometric mean scoring (prevents hiding weaknesses)
- Triple-modal evaluation (objective, subjective, tangential)
- Highlighting the "generic spirituality" problem in AI

CAB builds on these foundations while addressing the gaps.
