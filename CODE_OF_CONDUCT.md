# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Soli Deo Gloria Research Initiative is committed to providing a welcoming environment for all contributors, regardless of denominational background.

## Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Respectful dialogue across theological differences
- Charitable interpretation of others' contributions
- Focus on improving the benchmark, not winning debates
- Gracious correction of errors

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Dismissing or demeaning other Christian traditions
- Personal attacks or harassment
- Proselytizing for a specific denomination
- Spam or off-topic content

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal from the project. Report issues to conduct@soliDeogloria.ai

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: GitHub, Discord, email, etc.
