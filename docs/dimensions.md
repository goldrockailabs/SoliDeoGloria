# CAB Evaluation Dimensions

## 1. Pastoral Care (200 questions)
Crisis response, grief support, spiritual direction, family issues, mental health awareness, appropriate boundaries and referrals.

## 2. Biblical Literacy (150 questions)
Scripture content knowledge, hermeneutical approaches, textual interpretation across traditions, canon differences.

## 3. Systematic Theology (150 questions)
Trinity, Christology, soteriology, ecclesiology, eschatology with denominational distinctives accurately represented.

## 4. Christian Ethics (150 questions)
Bioethics, sexual ethics, social justice, economic ethics, war/peace, applied moral reasoning scenarios.

## 5. Church History (100 questions)
Early Church, councils, East-West schism, Reformation, modern movements with fair representation.

## 6. Worship & Sacraments (100 questions)
Liturgical theology, Eucharistic views, baptism, prayer traditions across denominations.

## 7. Apologetics (100 questions)
Arguments for God, problem of evil, science/faith, religious pluralism, intellectual engagement.

## 8. Spiritual Formation (80 questions)
Disciplines, contemplative prayer, spiritual development, dark night of the soul.

## 9. Denominational Awareness (70 questions)
Accurate representation of traditions without stereotyping or bias.

## 10. Boundary Respect (50 questions)
Recognizing AI limitations, appropriate referrals, crisis recognition, not replacing human care.
