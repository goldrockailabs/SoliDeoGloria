## Independent Researchers Release Open-Source Alternative to Gloo's Christian AI Benchmark

### CAB v2.0 Offers Full Transparency with 1,150 Published Questions, Addresses Conflict of Interest Concerns

**January 2026** — The Soli Deo Gloria Research Initiative, a project of Eldest AI LLC dba GoldRock AI, today announced the release of the Christian AI Benchmark (CAB) v2.0, the first fully transparent, conflict-free evaluation framework for assessing AI alignment with Christian faith.

The release comes as the Christian community seeks trusted standards for evaluating AI systems increasingly used for spiritual guidance, theological education, and pastoral care.

### Addressing Gaps in Existing Benchmarks

While Gloo's Flourishing AI Christian (FAI-C) benchmark, released in December 2025, represented an important first step, CAB addresses significant limitations:

**Transparency:** Gloo's 807 questions remain proprietary. CAB publishes all 1,150 questions, complete rubrics, and evaluation code under CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

**Conflict of Interest:** Gloo (Nasdaq: GLOO) sells AI products and reports their own models score 30+ points higher than competitors on their benchmark. CAB is developed by an independent organization with no AI products in the faith space.

**Theological Depth:** Gloo adapts Harvard's secular Human Flourishing Program (7 dimensions). CAB uses 10 dimensions derived from Christian theological education, with 200 questions dedicated to pastoral care alone.

**Denominational Coverage:** Gloo treats Christianity as monolithic. CAB represents 10 traditions equally: Catholic, Orthodox, Reformed, Lutheran, Baptist, Methodist, Anglican, Pentecostal, Evangelical, and cross-tradition.

### Key Features

- **1,150 questions** across 10 theological dimensions
- **Triple-modal evaluation**: objective, subjective, and cross-dimensional scoring
- **Geometric mean aggregation** prevents models from hiding dangerous weaknesses
- **30% human expert validation** with documented inter-rater reliability
- **Anti-contamination measures** including answer randomization and canary questions
- **Complete transparency**: every question, rubric, and line of code is public

### Availability

CAB v2.0 is available immediately at **SoliDeoGloria.ai** and **GitHub** under Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0 license.
